Suspension Fluid

FORK OIL 10W

SAE 10W Anti-Foam Anti-Wear

FORK OIL 10W is a high-performance motorcycle suspension fluid designed for both on-road and off-road applications. It provides consistent damping performance across a wide range of temperatures, ensuring a smooth and controlled ride. Formulated with superior anti-foam and anti-wear properties, it protects internal components and extends seal life.

Key Features

Consistent damping performance in all weather conditions
Superior anti-foam and aeration resistance
Excellent seal compatibility to prevent leaks
Reduces friction for smooth suspension action
Outstanding oxidation resistance for long service life
